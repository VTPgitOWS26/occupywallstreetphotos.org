Occupy Wall Street Photos

Between October 2011 and May 2012, Vance Petrunoff traveled across the United States documenting 18 Occupy encampments, from New York to California and Hawaii.

The archive documents demonstrations, handmade signs, community kitchens, libraries, assemblies, volunteers, music, art, and the everyday life of the movement.
