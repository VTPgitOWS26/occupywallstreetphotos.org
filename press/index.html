Occupy Wall Street Photos

A chronological, single-photographer documentary archive of the Occupy movement.

Between October 2011 and May 2012, photographer Vance Petrunoff documented 18 Occupy encampments across the United States, from New York to California and Hawaii.

For additional information, please download the press materials below. High-resolution photographs are available upon request.
