Occupy Wall Street Photos

A chronological, single-photographer documentary archive of the Occupy movement.

Between October 2011 and May 2012, Vance Petrunoff documented 18 Occupy encampments across the United States, from New York to California and Hawaii.

The photographs record demonstrations, handmade signs, community kitchens, libraries, assemblies, volunteers, music, art and the everyday life of the movement.
